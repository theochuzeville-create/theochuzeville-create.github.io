Hive Holdout

A hive defense game for Android

Bees against bugs

Ants, wasps and hornets follow a path to the hive. You place bees on the honeycomb cells along the path to stop them before they get there. The game is played in portrait mode, with one hand.

Coming soon to Google Play

What's in the game

  • Ten levels, each on its own map, playable in Easy, Normal or Hard, then an endless mode.
  • Six bees that unlock as you progress: the Guard hits hard up close, the Archer throws pollen from afar, the Nurse slows insects down, the Fanner blows them back, the Sentinel shoots from very far away and the Forager brings in honey.
  • Each bee is upgraded with honey. Past a certain tier you pick one of two branches, and the other one closes for that bee.
  • Bees gain experience as you play, which opens new upgrade tiers.
  • Eight kinds of insects, including two bosses, and an Insect Journal that keeps a card for each one you have met.
  • The game is available in English and French.

Free, with ads

The game is free and has no in-app purchases. It shows Google AdMob ads: a full-screen ad after every second finished game, never during play, and two optional videos (extra honey at the start, or a second chance after a defeat). There is no account to create and no game server: your scores and settings stay on your phone.
