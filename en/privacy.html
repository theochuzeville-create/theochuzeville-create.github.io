Privacy policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026.

This page covers the Android game Hive Holdout: Bee Defense, called Rempart de Miel in French (package name com.theochuzeville.rempartdemiel), published by Théo Chuzeville, an independent developer. Contact: theochuzeville@gmail.com.

In short. The developer does not collect any data about you: no account, no server, no analytics tool. Your scores and settings stay on your phone. The game shows Google AdMob ads, and Google collects data to serve them, under its own policy and, in Europe, according to your consent choice.

What the game keeps on your phone

The game stores the following on your phone, in its private storage:

  • your progress: unlocked levels, stars, best games, bees and insects already met, experience earned by the bees;
  • your settings: sounds, music, vibrations, language.

This information never leaves the phone and the developer has no access to it. It is erased when you uninstall the game or clear its data in the Android settings.

What the developer does not do

  • There is no account to create, and the game does not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • The game sends nothing to a server run by the developer: there isn't one.
  • The developer uses no analytics or tracking tool.
  • There are no in-app purchases.
  • The game does not access your contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera or GPS location.

Google AdMob ads

The game is free and funded by ads. It uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob), provided by Google. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the service is provided by Google Ireland Limited; elsewhere, by Google LLC.

To select, show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, the SDK automatically collects and sends to Google, according to Google's documentation:

  • the IP address, used to estimate the device's approximate location (country or city, not GPS location);
  • device identifiers: the Android advertising ID, the app set ID and other identifiers related to accounts signed in on the device;
  • interactions: app launches, taps, video views;
  • diagnostic information: launch time, hang rate, energy usage.

This data is encrypted in transit. The developer does not receive it: AdMob only shows him totals (number of ads shown, earnings).

The game asks Google for ads suitable for ages 13 and up (content rating "PG").

To learn what Google does with this data:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses cookies and advertising identifiers

Your consent and how to change it

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent form on first launch. You can accept or refuse personalised ads and the use of your data, all at once or per provider. If you refuse, the game stays fully playable: ads are still shown, but they are no longer personalised. Google may still process some data, for example to limit how often an ad repeats or to prevent fraud.

To change your mind at any time: open the pause menu during a game, then tap "Privacy / ad choices". The form needs an internet connection to open.

On your phone, you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in the Android settings (depending on the model: Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Android permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads. Without a connection the game works normally and the ad buttons are greyed out.
  • Vibration: when the hive is hit or a boss arrives. Vibrations can be turned off in the pause menu.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google's SDK, as described above.
  • A few technical permissions that Google's SDK declares itself. None of them gives access to your contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera or GPS location.

Children

The game is intended for people aged 13 and over. It is not designed for children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect any data about them. If you believe a child under 13 is using the game, you can uninstall it, and write to the contact address above with any question.

Your rights

The developer holds no data about you, so he cannot give you a copy of it or delete it. For advertising data, you can exercise your rights with Google, through the links above or in My Ad Center. In France, you can also file a complaint with the CNIL; elsewhere, with your local data protection authority.

Changes

If the game changes the way it handles data, for example when an online leaderboard is added, this page will be updated before that version is released, and its date will change.
