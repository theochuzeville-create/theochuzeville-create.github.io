Fact Vault uploader

A private tool for the Fact Vault YouTube channel

What this app is

Fact Vault is a YouTube channel of short educational videos (Shorts): one surprising fact about animals, the human body, space, history or everyday things, explained in under a minute.

The Fact Vault uploader is the private tool its owner, Théo Chuzeville, uses to run that channel. It is not offered to the public and nobody else can sign in to it. It only acts on the Fact Vault channel, which belongs to Théo. It:

  • uploads the Shorts and schedules their publication;
  • sets video titles and thumbnails;
  • posts one question as a comment under each new Short, and reads the public comments left on the channel's videos;
  • adds each Short to the channel's playlists;
  • reads the channel's YouTube Analytics statistics (views, watch time) to decide which topics to make next.
